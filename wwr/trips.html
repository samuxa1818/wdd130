Our Rafting Trips

Choose the rafting experience that matches your skill level and sense of adventure. From calm beginner-friendly routes to intense whitewater challenges, we have a trip for everyone.

Beginner River Adventure

Perfect for first-time rafters and families. This trip features calmer waters, smaller rapids, and plenty of time to enjoy the scenery while learning the basics of rafting with an experienced guide.

Intermediate Rapids Adventure

Designed for guests who want more excitement. This route includes stronger rapids, faster water, and more challenging sections while still being suitable for rafters with some experience or good physical condition.

Advanced Whitewater Challenge

Our most intense rafting experience. This trip features powerful rapids, technical sections, and fast-moving water. It is recommended for experienced rafters who are comfortable with demanding outdoor adventures.
